City Metro

The Lego City County Metropolitan Transportation Authority

A fictional transit authority I founded in 2022 — and the whole universe that's grown up around it since.

How it started

City Metro began as a Lego City-themed transit authority — a fictional agency built with the same seriousness as a real transit operator: route maps, branding, codes of conduct, the works. It's been my main ongoing creative project since 2022.

2022

City Metro (LCCMTA) founded. Felix takes on the role of founder and CEO.

2024

The Republic States of America expands the universe outward, adding a broader fictional setting around City Metro's services.

Ongoing

New sub-services, agencies, and systems continue to get added as the world grows.

What's inside the universe

City Metro Transit Police (CMTP)

A multi-page site for the fictional transit police force that keeps the system running safely.

Republic States of America

An eleven-page law enforcement megasite covering federal, state, and local fictional agencies, each with its own custom seal.

MyBrickPass (BrickPass)

A closed-loop fare system for City Metro — NFC cards, a Val100 validator, and SSO-linked rider accounts.

Customer Code of Conduct

A full policy document for riders, modeled structurally on LA Metro's own code of conduct.

Visitor mail system

A visitor email forwarding system running on Cloudflare Workers, KV storage, and Cloudflare's Email Routing API.

More on the way

City Metro keeps expanding — new sub-agencies and services get folded in as the project grows.
